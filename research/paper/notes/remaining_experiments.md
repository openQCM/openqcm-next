# Remaining experiments before submission (updated 2026-10-08, manuscript v0.2; = SI Section S11)

1. **Reference impedance analyser on the same crystal, same day, air and water, thru configuration.** Settles the absolute accuracy of f_res, Γ and R_m (only shifts against theory were tested) and gives the reference's own φ (expected ≈ 0), i.e. whether the −8…−32° is the instrument's.
2. **Dedicated air → water acquisition with raw sweeps on both instruments**, liquid temperature logged at the interface, repeated on two days. Settles the reproducibility of Experiment 1 without the confounding of a change of board, crystal, software and year (today DS-2 vs DS-3 differ by 1–12 % in frequency on n = 3–9).
3. **Two boards × two sensors, air and water, overtones 1–9.** Settles whether φ(n) is a board constant (today: 1–5° between boards in air, non-monotonic on one) and whether the fundamental's bandwidth excess (+29…+37 % on the 2026 crystal, +6.5 % on the 2024 one) follows the sensor or the board.
4. **Liquid temperature measured, or a water–glycerol series at controlled temperature with tabulated ρη.** Settles the ±2–3 % of the KG prediction left open by the unmeasured temperature and tests KG-3 over a range of √(ρη); adopting literature ρη for the glucose solutions at 25 °C turns the relative KG-3 of Experiment 3 into an absolute one.
5. **Bench calibration of the phase channel**: a known phase ramp through 0° (δ and the fold rounding) and an RLC standard in the operating range (0.5–5 kΩ) for φ_b(f). Settles the physical origin of φ and of its air–liquid difference at the fundamental.
6. **Long plateau (≥ 1 h) in water with the fit running live beside the conductance maximum.** Settles drift, noise, the fallback rate under the gate (0/120 offline) and the two-state behaviour of the 3rd overtone.
7. **Larger or switchable divider resistor (0.5–1 kΩ) for liquids.** Settles whether the 5–18 % out-of-roundness of the locus and the residual frequency excess come from operating the AD8302 at −22…−37 dB.
8. **Sweep window scaled with Γ (±6 Γ).** Removes the clipping of the ±3 Γ fit window for n ≥ 5 in isopropanol and n = 9 in water, and the baseline-on-skirt bias of the half-height width.
9. **Re-acquisition with firmware 0.1.5d** (no carry-over) and, for the concentration series, **a return to water between solutions and a repeated 5 % point** with the liquid temperature logged. Isolates the 0.6 % w/v offset of the 5 % plateau (instrument step vs first pour).
10. **A third liquid (20–40 % glycerol) and a viscoelastic film.** Tests the estimator where r_n ≠ 1 is expected.
